## The Game Identified

**Transaction-primary, reputation-compounding.**

We sell engagements, subscriptions, and cohorts — every revenue unit is a discrete purchase. But unlike pure SaaS, every successful engagement is *also* an asset: case studies, referrals, testimonials, founder authority. The NSM must capture both the transaction *and* whether it compounds. A consultancy that books transactions but doesn't deliver outcomes dies in 18–24 months — the referral engine breaks. Quality is the floor, not the ceiling.

## North Star Metric (NSM)

### 🌟 "Compounding Clients" (CC)

**Definition:** Number of clients who, in any rolling 12-month period, have done at least *two* of the following:

- Engaged in an initial paid offering (Strategic Sprint, Foundation Build, or OS Install)
- Are actively paying for ongoing Notion OS subscription
- Have re-engaged for a second offering within 12 months
- Have referred at least one new client who converted to a paid engagement
- Have produced a public case study, testimonial, or usable quote

**Why this works:** It's not enough to win an engagement — that's a transaction, not a compound. A Compounding Client is one who *demonstrably came back, paid forward, or amplified the brand.* Every CC is worth ~3x a flat client because they generate a second revenue stream, a referral, or a sales asset.

**Why this matters for The Practice specifically:** A founder-led consultancy has a hard delivery ceiling (~15 engagements/year solo). CC growth is the only mechanism that breaks the ceiling without hiring — each compounding client multiplies founder leverage by producing new pipeline or recurring revenue.

**Shorthand:** *"Clients who multiply us, not just clients who pay us."*

## Input Metrics (The Formula)

> **CC = f(Breadth × Conversion × Quality × Compounding)**
> 

### 1. 🎯 Breadth — Audits Sent Per Week

*Dimension: Breadth | Owner: Founder + CMO function*

Number of personalized Product Strategy Audits sent to qualified targets per week. **Target: 5+ sustained.** Top-of-funnel for the entire revenue engine. Lower than 3/week and pipeline starves; higher than 10/week without help and founder burns out.

### 2. 🔄 Conversion — Audit → Engagement Signed Rate

*Dimension: Efficiency | Owner: Founder*

Full-funnel conversion from audit sent to paid engagement signed. **Target: 15–25%** (3–4 of every 20 audits should close). This is the master GTM metric. A higher rate means targeting is sharper. A lower rate means audits are landing but the discovery call isn't converting — a different problem entirely.

### 3. ⭐ Quality — Client Outcome Score at 60 Days Post-Engagement

*Dimension: Quality (floor metric) | Owner: HoP function*

Structured 60-day post-engagement check-in that measures whether the methodology delivered observable change in the client's product org. Could be NPS-style or structured rubric covering: cycle time, feature adoption, financial visibility, team clarity. **Target: 8/10 average.** This is the floor — if it drops below 7, stop selling and fix delivery.

### 4. 🔁 Compounding — Referral Rate per Completed Engagement

*Dimension: Depth | Owner: Founder + CMO function*

% of completed engagements that produce a referred lead within 90 days. **Target: 40%+.** This is what separates a flat consultancy from a compounding one. Below 30% and the business is a treadmill; above 50% and the brand starts pulling pipeline without active outreach.

### 5. 🏠 Stickiness — Notion OS Active Usage at 90 Days Post-Engagement

*Dimension: Depth | Owner: HoP function*

% of post-engagement clients still actively using the Notion OS at 90 days. **Target: 60%+.** If clients abandon the OS, the recurring revenue tail dies and the brand promise of "installed methodology" is exposed as theater.

## Lagging Indicators

CC predicts these mid/long-term business outcomes:

- **Annual revenue** (target Year 1: $300–500K; Year 2: $700K–$1M; with facilitator program Year 3: $1.5–$2.5M)
- **Pipeline cost** — CC growth lowers acquisition cost over time (referrals are nearly free)
- **Founder time leverage** — CC creates the conditions for the facilitator program (Phase 3 unlock)
- **Brand authority signals** — inbound podcast invites, speaking gigs, book deals
- **Methodology durability** — case studies and outcome data validate the methodology beyond the founder's personal reputation

## Session Notes — Key Tensions

1. **Quality is the floor, not the ceiling** — a consultancy that maximizes audits-sent but lets quality slip kills itself in 18–24 months. The HoP perspective is non-negotiable in the formula.
2. **Founder time is the binding constraint** — every input metric is in service of either generating revenue per founder hour OR creating the conditions for the facilitator program that breaks the ceiling.
3. **CC is a long-cycle metric** — measurable at quarterly intervals, not weekly. Don't optimize for CC directly; optimize the five inputs and let CC follow.
