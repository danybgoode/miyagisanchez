## The Game Identified

**Productivity-primary, Transaction-validated.**

The product's core mechanism is Productivity — replacing manual, repetitive multichannel operations with automated, intelligent workflows. Merchants are not supposed to spend more time in Bonsai; they're supposed to spend less time on everything Bonsai handles. However, the ultimate value proof is commercial (Transaction): more sales, higher margins, better GMV per channel. The North Star must measure operational value delivery, but must be cross-checked against a commercial lagging indicator. If operational efficiency rises without merchant GMV improvement, something is broken in the value chain.

## North Star Metric (NSM)

### 🌟 "Deeply Active Merchants" (DAM)

**Definition:** The number of merchants who, in any given 30-day period, meet ALL three of the following conditions:

1. **3 or more channels** connected and actively syncing
2. **AI agents have handled 20+ customer interactions** across at least 2 channels
3. **At least 1 operational rule** (pricing, shipping, or financial reporting) actively firing

**Why this works:** It is not enough to have channels connected (passive setup). It is not enough to have conversations (could be a single busy channel). It is not enough to have rules configured (could be set-and-ignored). A merchant who hits all three thresholds has structurally integrated Bonsai into their daily operation. That merchant has made Bonsai the operating system of their ecommerce. They are not churning.

**Shorthand:** *"Merchants who've made Bonsai the operating system of their ecommerce."*

**The Netflix analogy:** Like Netflix discovering that members who queued 3+ DVDs in their first session were significantly more likely to retain, DAM is the behavioral threshold that separates surface adopters from structurally integrated merchants.

## Input Metrics (The Formula)

> **DAM = f(Breadth × Depth × Frequency × Efficiency)**
> 

### 1. 🌐 Breadth — Channels Connected at Day 30

*Dimension: Breadth | Owner: CEO / Onboarding*

How many channels does a merchant have active and syncing by the end of their first month? **Target: 3+.** A merchant with only 1–2 channels connected has not yet reached the operational complexity where Bonsai's consolidation value is truly felt. Breadth drives the conditions for DAM.

### 2. 🤖 Depth — Agent Resolution Rate

*Dimension: Depth | Owner: CMO / Product*

Percentage of customer conversations resolved by AI agent without human escalation. **Target: 70%+.** A low resolution rate means the agent is not trained well enough or not trusted by the merchant team — they'll revert to manual handling, undermining the core value prop and eventually churning.

### 3. ⚡ Frequency — Weekly Agent Conversations per Merchant

*Dimension: Frequency | Owner: CEO / CMO*

Volume of real customer interactions handled by AI agents per merchant per week. Low frequency signals the merchant is not routing real traffic through agents yet — surface integration only, not operational integration. Rising frequency = merchant trust in the system is growing.

### 4. ⏱ Efficiency — Time-to-DAM

*Dimension: Efficiency | Owner: CMO / CTO / Onboarding*

Days from merchant signup to hitting the Deeply Active Merchant threshold. **Target: under 14 days.** This is the team's most actionable lever — better onboarding flows, faster channel connections, smarter agent setup, and proactive agency partner coaching all compress this number. It maps directly to the PMF Workshop 1 goal of "time-to-first-win under 14 days."

### 5. 🔧 System Health — Channel Sync Error Rate

*Dimension: Efficiency (floor metric) | Owner: CTO / Engineering*

Percentage of channel sync operations completing without error. **Target: below 2%.** This is the floor metric — if channels aren't syncing cleanly, no input metric above is meaningful. Must be green before any growth lever is pulled. Monitored continuously with merchant-facing alerting before they notice problems.

## Lagging Indicators

The NSM (DAM) predicts these mid/long-term business results:

- **Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)** — DAM merchants don't churn; each represents $200–600 USD/month recurring
- **Net Revenue Retention (NRR)** — DAM merchants expand (add channels, upgrade tiers); NRR above 110% is the goal
- **LTV** — At $400/month ACV and <3% monthly churn, DAM merchants generate ~$13,300 LTV each
- **Merchant GMV influenced by Bonsai** — The commercial validation that Productivity gains translate to Transaction outcomes
- **Agency partner NPS** — Leading indicator of referral pipeline health

## Session Notes — Key Tensions Surfaced

1. **Productivity vs. Transaction game** — The delivery is Productivity; the value proof is Transaction. NSM bridges both by using operational behavior (agent activity, rule firing) that leads to commercial outcomes.
2. **Volume vs. Quality** — High agent conversation volume with low resolution rate is a danger signal, not a success signal. Frequency and Depth must be read together.
3. **The threshold hypothesis** — The specific DAM thresholds (3 channels, 20 conversations, 1 rule) are hypotheses that must be validated against real retention data once the merchant base is large enough. Expect refinement at ~50 merchants.
