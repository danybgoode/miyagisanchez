## Selected Validation Technique: Parallel Digital Acquisition Tests (17-day sprint)

**Why this technique:** Pre-revenue, pre-product consultancy. The methodology and founder exist. The binding constraint on validation is not time or money — it’s isolating which acquisition channel and which message converts the right buyer. Parallel digital tests answer that question in 17 days, not 6 weeks, and without relying on any single channel or personal relationship.

**The funnel:**

> Marketing site → Health Diagnostic → Engagement Application → Discovery Call → Signed Engagement
> 

**Three channels, run simultaneously for 14 days:**

**Channel A — LinkedIn Organic**

4 posts/week from founder account. Mix: insight posts, short workshop narratives, artifact shares. Drives traffic to the diagnostic. Cost: time only.

**Channel B — LinkedIn Paid**

$1.5K budget, 14 days. Narrow targeting: Founders/CEOs/CPOs at SaaS companies 50–300 employees. 3 ad variants (pain hook / outcome hook / curiosity hook). Kill underperformers at Day 7, reallocate to top performer.

**Channel C — One Gated Live Session**

Day 9. 45-minute faceless live build session (A/B test implementation with Claude Code on a sample app). Application required, 30 seats capped. Promoted only via Channels A and B. Recording becomes a Channel A asset afterward.

## Go/No-Go Criteria

| Signal | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| 3 engagements signed at $15K+ | **VALIDATED.** Scale the winning channels. Activate Phase 1. |
| 1–2 signed (not 3) | Extend 7 days. Focus on closing existing pipeline. No new acquisition spend. |
| Applications received, 0 closed | VP or pricing issue. Interview every no before retesting. |
| Diagnostic completions, 0 applications | Funnel friction or wrong audience. Fix the application page or targeting. |
| 0 diagnostic completions | Messaging or targeting broken. Stop. Do not increase spend. Fix messaging first. |

## Quality Metric

**Financial Outcome Score** (replaces NPS):

Before engagement: baseline 3–5 KPIs agreed with client (cycle time, R&D ROI, % of shipped features generating revenue, etc.).

At 30 and 60 days post-engagement: re-measure same KPIs against baseline AND against industry benchmark.

This is the case study asset that unlocks all downstream scaling.
