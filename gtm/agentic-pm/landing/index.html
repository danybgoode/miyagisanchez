03 — Why product managers

Product managers have been writing prompts for 15 years.
They called them tickets.

User stories, acceptance criteria, Gherkin: instructions precise enough for a machine, written long before agents could read them. Now they can. So the person who decides what to build can also build it, measure it and report it, without the ticket ping-pong.
